Notice & wonder

As you move the plane, where do contours split, join, or shrink? Rotate the surface and compare those places with the map. Which might be a local maximum, a local minimum, or a saddle point?

Shared window: −2.2 ≤ x ≤ 2.2, −1.8 ≤ y ≤ 1.8. Curves are numerical approximations; tiny loops or exact touching points may be hard to see. The plane is translucent so the surface stays visible.

Explore without the plots

Use the plane slider with arrow keys, or the plus and minus buttons. A contour is the set of coordinates satisfying 3x − x³ − 2y² + y⁴ = c. To reason about the surface, compare the height at nearby coordinates in different directions: does it increase, decrease, or do both? The map inspection below gives a numerical height.
